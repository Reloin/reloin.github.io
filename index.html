About

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, where my research focuses on the safety of AI agents and vision-language-action (VLA) models. My interest in agents goes back to my undergraduate thesis at Tsinghua University, where I built a Minecraft agent driven by multimodal foundation models. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a test and research engineer at IME, A*STAR, building automated wafer test systems from the ground up, and I have also gained hands-on experience with embedded IoT hardware.

Research

Final Year Thesis

Game Agent Control Based on Visual Language Foundation Model

Inspired by Ghost in the Minecraft and Voyager, my final-year thesis built a general-purpose agent that autonomously explores and interacts with the Minecraft environment using an open-source multimodal foundation model. LLaVA (Large Language and Vision Assistant) generates tasks from visual and textual prompts, while LLaMA (Large Language Model Meta AI) works alongside it to carry out the generated steps.

Mar 2023 — Jun 2023

Game Interaction on Non-human Primates

This project analyzed marmoset brain activity patterns during interactive behavioral tasks. I engineered an algorithm that generates mazes and quantifies their complexity, combining mathematical modeling with built-in maze visualization.
